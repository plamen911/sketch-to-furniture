## Observed conventions

- A freehand perspective shows the front, right side and top. Perspective lines are illustrative, not a reliable source of scale or angles. The written dimensions govern.
- Solid front outlines identify doors and drawer fronts; dashed internal horizontals identify hidden shelves. The small horizontal element high in the right bay is a clothes rail. Handle strokes suggest placement, not exact hardware or mounting dimensions.
- Dimensions are in millimetres. Outer width 1200, overall height 2200 and depth 600. The paired 600 labels are nominal bay widths, not 600 mm clear spaces. Account for all 18 mm walls and the center divider.
- The vertical chain 1700 + 200 + 200 + 100 closes to 2200. The client confirmed these as upper zone, two stacked left drawers, and plinth. These nominal bands do not define finished front dimensions after gaps.
- The drawing's lower support symbols look like feet, but the subsequent written answer says “цокъл 100 mm”. For this project follow the written answer, retain the visual discrepancy in assumptions, and do not generalize every future support symbol as a closed plinth.
- Material annotations specify particleboard for the carcass and cream MDF AGT 730A for fronts. The client later specified white 18 mm particleboard and white 3 mm HDF back. A finish code does not supply an exact sRGB value or texture.
- Clarifications fixed three left shelves with four equal clear openings above the drawer zone, and two right lower shelves with equal clear gaps below a 1100 mm hanging space. The client explicitly clarified that 1100 is **clear height from shelf top to upper boundary underside**, not rail-axis height.

## Before interpreting the next sketch

Use these conventions as hypotheses. Transcribe actual numbers, reconcile dimension chains, and compare solid/dashed elements to the client's answers. Carry over only explicitly persistent choices; the wardrobe's geometry, colors and shelf counts are project-specific. Ask about a remaining contradiction only after checking existing answers, and never ask the same resolved question again.
